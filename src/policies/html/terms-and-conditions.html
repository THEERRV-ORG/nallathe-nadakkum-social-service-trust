Who we are

Emergency ambulance — read this first

In a life-threatening emergency, call 108 or 112 first.

Our ambulance is a charity service with one vehicle. It is not a substitute for the State emergency service and must not be your only plan.

The service is free, but reserved for families below the poverty line who cannot afford private ambulance charges. Eligibility is checked on the call, availability depends on the vehicle being free, and transfers focus on Government Hospitals. It is not an advanced life support ambulance. We act in good faith and cannot be held responsible for clinical outcomes, delays or traffic.

Asking for help

Every request is physically verified by our volunteers before we allocate resources. Please give accurate information and any documents we ask for. Submitting a request does not guarantee assistance — we help according to need, eligibility and what we have available. Emergency cases are prioritised. Giving false information to obtain charitable help is a criminal offence and takes help away from someone who needs it.

Volunteering

  • Volunteering is unpaid and creates no employment relationship or entitlement to wages.
  • You must be 18 or over to volunteer independently.
  • All volunteers must follow our Child Protection Policy and keep beneficiaries' details confidential.
  • Volunteers must not raise funds, speak for the Trust or make promises to beneficiaries without written authorisation.
  • Field work can involve travel, physical activity and distressing situations. You take part at your own risk, and we do not currently provide volunteer insurance.
