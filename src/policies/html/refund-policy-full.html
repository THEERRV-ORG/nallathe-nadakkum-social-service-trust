1. Introduction

Nallathae Nadakkum Samuga Sevai Arakkattalai ("Nallathae Nadakkum", "the Trust", "we", "us" or "our") is a registered public charitable trust (Registration No. 16/2025) based in Tiruchengode, Tamil Nadu, India. We operate the website at Nallathae-nadakkum-social-service-tr.vercel.app (the "Website").

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains what happens if a donor wishes to cancel a contribution, or believes a payment was made in error. It applies to all contributions received through or in connection with the Website, including payments made using the QR code or UPI ID displayed on the Website, and to material donations offered to the Trust.

Please read this Policy together with our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

2. Our General Position on Refunds

Donations to Nallathae Nadakkum are voluntary, charitable and generally non-refundable.

This is not an arbitrary restriction. The Trust operates with 0% administrative deduction and deploys contributions almost immediately into daily field work - cooked meals distributed the same morning, municipal fees paid at the time of a burial, diesel purchased for an emergency hospital run, or tuition fees remitted directly to a school. Once funds have been committed in this way they cannot be recovered, and a refund would have to come out of money intended for another person in need.

In short: a donation is a gift, not a purchase. You do not receive goods or services in exchange, so there is no transaction to reverse in the ordinary commercial sense.

We do, however, recognise that genuine mistakes happen. Section 4 sets out the limited circumstances in which we will consider a refund, and we deal with such requests fairly and promptly.

3. How Donations Are Currently Received

At present the Website does not have an integrated payment gateway. Contributions are accepted through:

  • a QR code and a UPI ID displayed on the Website;
  • direct bank transfer to the Trust's account, where those details are shared with you by an authorised representative; and
  • material contributions handed over in person or delivered to our distribution centre.

When you donate by UPI, the payment is completed entirely within your own UPI or banking application. The Website does not host a payment page, does not process the payment, and does not store card details, banking credentials, UPI PINs or one-time passwords. This has two practical consequences for refunds:

  • We have no ability to reverse a UPI payment automatically. There is no gateway dashboard through which a transaction can be voided. Any approved refund must be paid out manually by the Trust as a fresh transfer.
  • We may not become aware of your payment until it appears in the Trust's bank statement, and we may not be able to identify you from it. If you want your contribution acknowledged - or later queried - please tell us about it using the details in Section 20.

4. When a Refund May Be Considered

The Trust will consider a refund request where the donor can reasonably demonstrate one of the following:

SituationWhat we will do
Duplicate payment - the same amount was paid twice or more within a short period, in error. We will verify against the Trust's bank statement and refund the excess amount.
Incorrect amount - a clear data-entry error, such as Rs. 50,000 paid instead of Rs. 5,000. Where the error is evident and verifiable, we will refund the excess. The intended amount will be retained as your donation unless you ask otherwise.
Unauthorised payment - the payment was made from your account without your knowledge or consent. We will refund in full on verification, and cooperate with your bank and the authorities. See Section 14.
Technical error - the amount was debited more than once for a single intended payment because of a banking or network fault. We will refund the excess. Where the fault lies with the bank or UPI network, we will also direct you to the correct channel.
Payment intended for another recipient - funds reached the Trust's account by genuine mistake. We will return the amount on verification.

4.1 Time limit

A refund request must reach us within seven (7) calendar days of the date of the transaction. Because contributions are deployed into daily field work almost immediately, requests received after this period cannot ordinarily be considered.

We may relax this limit at our discretion in exceptional cases - for example, a proven unauthorised transaction discovered later, or a bereavement or medical emergency that prevented timely contact. Any such decision is made case by case and does not create a precedent.

4.2 Discretion

All refund decisions rest with the Trustees of Nallathae Nadakkum and are made in good faith, having regard to the verified facts, the interests of our beneficiaries, and our obligations under Indian law. We will give you our reasons in writing.

5. When a Refund Will Not Be Made

Refunds will not ordinarily be made in the following circumstances:

  • A change of mind after a validly made and intended donation.
  • Dissatisfaction with how a programme was carried out, or with the Trust's operational decisions, priorities or public communications.
  • Requests made after the seven-day window set out in Section 4.1, save in the exceptional cases described there.
  • Funds already deployed - for example, where a meal session has been served, a burial performed, fuel purchased, or a tuition fee remitted to a school on the strength of your contribution.
  • Donations made toward a specific verified case - such as an emergency medical fundraiser or a last-rites case - once the case has been settled or the funds released.
  • Material donations, which are governed by Section 10.
  • Blood donations and volunteer time, which by their nature cannot be refunded. See Section 11.
  • Anonymous contributions that we cannot reliably trace to you, or where identity and payment details cannot be verified.
  • Payments made to any account, QR code or link not officially published by the Trust. See Section 14.

The Trust also reserves the right to decline or return a contribution at its discretion - for example, where the source of funds is unclear, where acceptance would conflict with our values or legal obligations, or where the donation is offered on conditions the Trust cannot meet.

6. How to Request a Refund

Write to us at +91 75400 17625 with the subject line "Refund Request", or WhatsApp +91 75400 17625. A written request is strongly preferred, as it allows us to verify the details accurately.

Please include the following:

  1. Your full name and the mobile number or email address you can be reached on;
  2. The date and time of the transaction;
  3. The amount paid;
  4. The UPI transaction ID / UTR / reference number, or a screenshot of the payment confirmation from your app;
  5. The UPI ID or bank account from which the payment was made;
  6. A brief explanation of why a refund is being sought, and which situation in Section 4 applies;
  7. Where relevant, the programme you intended to support.

Never send us your UPI PIN, card number, CVV, net-banking password or any one-time password (OTP). We will never ask for these, and no representative of the Trust is authorised to request them. A transaction reference number and a payment screenshot are all we need.

We may ask for reasonable additional proof - such as a bank statement entry showing the debit - before approving a refund. This protects genuine donors from fraudulent claims made in their name.

7. How Refunds Are Processed

Where a refund is approved:

  • We will acknowledge your request within three (3) working days of receiving it.
  • We will communicate our decision within seven (7) working days of receiving all the information we have asked for.
  • Approved refunds will be transferred within fourteen (14) working days of approval.
  • Refunds are made only to the original source - the same UPI ID or bank account from which the payment was received. We will not remit a refund to a third-party account, a different name, or a cash payout.
  • Refunds are made in Indian Rupees for the amount actually received by the Trust.
  • No interest is payable on any refunded amount.
  • Any bank charges, gateway fees, foreign-exchange conversion losses or transfer costs already incurred and not recoverable by the Trust may be deducted from the refunded amount, and will be itemised for you.

Once the Trust has initiated the transfer, the time taken for the money to appear in your account depends on your bank and the UPI or banking network, and is outside our control.

8. Failed and Pending Transactions

If an amount was debited from your account but the payment failed, was declined, or does not appear to have reached the Trust, the funds have most likely not left the banking system and are normally auto-reversed by your bank, typically within three to seven working days.

In such cases, please raise the matter first with your bank or UPI application provider, which holds the transaction record. The Trust cannot refund an amount it has not received.

If the amount is not reversed and your bank confirms it was credited to the Trust, contact us with the transaction reference and we will verify against our bank statement and act under Section 4.

Unresolved UPI disputes may also be escalated through the grievance mechanism of your bank or payment service provider, and thereafter under the Reserve Bank of India's Integrated Ombudsman Scheme.

9. Cancelling a Pledge, Sponsorship or Recurring Commitment

A pledge - a promise to contribute that has not yet been paid - may be cancelled at any time before payment, with no obligation and no explanation required. Simply inform us by phone or WhatsApp at +91 75400 17625.

Where you have committed to an ongoing sponsorship - such as a student's annual tuition, an elder's monthly shelter contribution, or a recurring meal session:

  • You may discontinue future contributions at any time by writing to us. We ask for reasonable notice, ideally fifteen (15) days, so that we can make alternative arrangements for the person being supported.
  • Contributions already made and deployed are not refundable. Once a school fee has been remitted or a care-home deposit paid, that money is with the institution, not with the Trust.
  • Where a sponsorship is discontinued mid-cycle and funds have been received but not yet disbursed, we will discuss the options with you. The amount may be returned under Section 4 if within the applicable window, or redirected to a comparable purpose with your agreement.
  • Ending a sponsorship carries no penalty and no obligation to explain. We ask only for notice, so that no child or elder is left without support without warning.

Since the Website does not currently support automated recurring payments, any standing instruction or auto-pay mandate you have set up sits with your own bank or UPI application, and must be cancelled by you through that application. The Trust cannot cancel a mandate on your behalf.

10. Material Donations

The Trust accepts material contributions such as dhotis, sarees, shirts and school uniforms, rice bags, oil and groceries, notebooks and educational materials, and similar goods.

Material donations, once handed over to the Trust, cannot be returned, exchanged or converted into a monetary refund. Such goods are sorted and distributed to beneficiaries quickly, and are often in a recipient's hands within days.

Before donating goods, please note:

  • We accept clothing that is new or gently used - not heavily worn, torn or stained - so that it can be given with dignity.
  • Food items and groceries must be unopened, unexpired and fit for consumption, with adequate shelf life remaining.
  • The Trust may decline any material donation that is unsuitable, unsafe, unhygienic, unusable, or not needed at the time.
  • Goods that cannot be used as intended may be redirected to another appropriate charitable purpose or responsibly disposed of, at the Trust's discretion.
  • Transport and delivery costs for material donations are borne by the donor unless separately agreed.
  • If goods are declined at the point of handover, they remain yours and will be returned to you at that time. Once accepted, they become the property of the Trust.

Please contact us before sending goods, so that we can confirm current requirements and avoid waste.

11. Blood Donation and Volunteering

Blood donation. Registering as a voluntary blood donor creates no financial obligation and involves no payment, so no question of refund arises. You may withdraw your registration or decline any individual request at any time, for any reason or none. Blood is donated only at a licensed blood bank or hospital, subject to that institution's medical screening and eligibility criteria. A donation of blood, once given, cannot be recalled.

Volunteering. Volunteer time and service are given freely and are not refundable or compensable. You may withdraw from a volunteer commitment at any time; we ask only that you tell us as early as you can, so that meal routes, transport and field work are not left short-handed.

12. Donation Receipts and Tax Status

12.1 Current tax status of the Trust

Donations to Nallathae Nadakkum are not currently tax-deductible. The Trust was registered on 8 April 2025, and applications for registration under Sections 12A and 80G of the Income-tax Act, 1961 are pending with the Income Tax Department.

Until those registrations are formally granted, the Trust cannot issue 80G certificates or tax-deductible receipts, and no donor should claim a deduction in respect of a contribution made to the Trust. We state this openly at the point of giving rather than leaving donors to discover it at the time of filing.

What we do issue is a donation acknowledgement - a written confirmation recording your name, the amount, the date and the programme supported. This is a record of your contribution and of our gratitude. It is not a tax certificate and must not be presented as one.

If and when 12A and 80G registration is granted, this Policy and our receipting practice will be updated, and the position will be published on our Transparency & Legal page. Registration, when granted, will apply from the date specified by the Income Tax Department and will not retrospectively make earlier donations deductible unless the department's order so provides.

12.2 Effect of a refund on receipts

Where a donation is refunded in whole or in part:

  • Any acknowledgement or receipt issued for the refunded amount stands cancelled with effect from the date of refund, and must not be relied on for any purpose.
  • The Trust will make the corresponding correction in its books of account, which are maintained as a complete ledger and audited annually by a certified Chartered Accountant.
  • If, at the time of a future refund, the Trust holds 80G registration and a certificate has already been issued, you must return or destroy that certificate and must not claim the deduction. Where a claim has already been made, it is your responsibility to revise your return accordingly.
  • A refund may not be possible after the Trust has closed and audited its accounts for the financial year in which the donation was received, or after any statutory statement of donations for that period has been filed, as reversal at that stage would affect filings already made. This is a further reason to raise any concern promptly.

The Trust does not provide tax advice. Please consult your own tax adviser about the treatment of any contribution or refund in your return.

13. Foreign Contributions

The Trust does not hold registration under the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act, 2010 (FCRA) and is therefore legally prohibited from accepting any donation from a bank account located outside India.

We ask our international supporters to respect this legal boundary. Please do not remit funds from an overseas account. If you wish to support our work from abroad, please contact us first and we will explain what is lawfully possible.

If a foreign contribution nonetheless reaches the Trust's account, it will not be treated as a donation. The amount will be quarantined and returned to the source, or otherwise dealt with as required by law, and the Trust will make any regulatory report required of it. Such a return is not a "refund" under this Policy and is not subject to the seven-day window in Section 4.1 - it will be made whenever the receipt comes to light.

Return of a foreign contribution is subject to FCRA and applicable exchange-control regulations, may require prior reporting, and may take considerably longer than the timelines in Section 7. Exchange-rate differences and remittance charges are borne by the remitter.

This section will be updated if the Trust obtains FCRA registration or prior permission in future.

14. Fraudulent, Unauthorised and Misdirected Payments

Please contribute only through the QR code and UPI ID published on our official Website at Nallathae-nadakkum-social-service-tr.vercel.app, or through details given to you directly by an authorised representative of the Trust. Always verify the beneficiary name shown in your payment application before confirming a transaction.

We will never ask you to transfer money to an unlisted personal bank account or a private phone number. Every legitimate contribution goes to the Trust's official account, which operates under a joint bank mandate requiring the signatures of both the Chairman (Advocate N. Kavinraj) and the Treasurer (Mrs. S. Kokila). No single trustee can withdraw or dispense public donations alone.

The Trust is not responsible for, and cannot refund, money paid to any account, UPI ID, QR code, link or collection page that we have not officially published. Such payments never reach us and are beyond our control.

We maintain zero tolerance for financial discrepancies. If you encounter any page, message, social media account or individual soliciting funds in our name, report it immediately to +91 75400 17625 or WhatsApp +91 75400 17625 so that we can act - and please do so before paying if you are unsure whether an appeal is genuine.

If you believe a payment to the Trust was made from your account without your authorisation:

  1. Report it to your bank immediately - prompt reporting protects your rights under Reserve Bank of India rules on unauthorised electronic transactions.
  2. Inform us at once at +91 75400 17625 or +91 75400 17625 so that we can hold the amount pending verification rather than deploy it.
  3. We will cooperate fully with your bank, the National Payments Corporation of India and law enforcement, and will refund a verified unauthorised payment in full.

Cybercrime may also be reported to the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal at cybercrime.gov.in or on helpline 1930.

The Trust reserves the right to decline or return any contribution where the source of funds cannot be satisfactorily established, or where acceptance may contravene applicable law.

15. Future Payment Gateway and Online Features

This Policy is written to remain valid as the Website develops. If in future we introduce a payment gateway, online donation forms, recurring giving, event registration or ticketing, membership or a donor portal:

  • payments will be processed by a PCI-DSS compliant payment service provider regulated in India, and approved refunds will be routed back through the original payment method in accordance with that provider's timelines, typically five to ten working days after initiation;
  • the Trust will not store full card numbers, CVV, UPI PINs, net-banking credentials or one-time passwords;
  • gateway or transaction charges already deducted by the payment provider may not be recoverable and may be deducted from the refunded amount, and this will be disclosed to you;
  • recurring mandates will be cancellable both by written request to the Trust and directly through the donor's own bank or UPI application;
  • where an event or paid registration is introduced, specific cancellation terms will be published on that event's page and will prevail over this Policy for that event;
  • this Policy will be updated before or at the time any such feature goes live.
