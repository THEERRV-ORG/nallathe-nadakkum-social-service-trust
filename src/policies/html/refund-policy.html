Refund & Cancellation Policy

Nallathe Nadakkum Social Trust  ·  Last updated 28 July 2026

Donations are generally non-refundable — but if you made a genuine mistake, tell us within 7 days and we will sort it out.

Why donations are not usually refunded

We operate with 0% administrative deduction, and money is spent almost immediately — meals cooked the same morning, municipal fees paid at a burial, diesel for an emergency hospital run, a school fee remitted directly. Once that is done, it cannot be recovered, and refunding it would take money away from someone else in need.

A donation is a gift, not a purchase. You receive nothing in exchange, so there is no transaction to reverse in the usual sense.

When we will refund

We will refund if you can show one of these:

  • you paid twice by mistake;
  • you entered the wrong amount — for example ₹50,000 instead of ₹5,000;
  • the payment was made from your account without your permission;
  • a technical fault debited you more than once;
  • the money was meant for someone else and reached us by mistake.

Tell us within 7 days of the payment. After that the money has usually been spent. We can make exceptions for proven unauthorised transactions or genuine emergencies, decided case by case.

When we cannot refund

Simply changing your mind, disagreeing with how we run a programme, or asking after the money has already been spent on a meal session, burial, fuel or school fee. We also cannot refund material donations, blood donations or volunteer time.

How to ask

Email nallathanadakum@gmail.com with the subject "Refund Request", or call +91 75400 17625. Please include:

  • your name and contact number;
  • the date and amount;
  • the UPI transaction ID or a screenshot of the payment;
  • what went wrong.

Never send us your UPI PIN, OTP, card number or password. We will never ask for them. A transaction ID and screenshot are all we need.

What happens next

  • We acknowledge your request within 3 working days.
  • We tell you our decision within 7 working days.
  • Approved refunds are transferred within 14 working days.
  • Refunds go only to the account the money came from — never to a different account or in cash. No interest is payable.

If your payment failed

If money left your account but never reached us, it is usually auto-reversed by your bank within 3–7 working days. Please contact your bank first — we cannot refund money we never received. If your bank confirms it reached us, send us the reference and we will check.

Cancelling a pledge or sponsorship

You can cancel an unpaid pledge any time, no explanation needed. If you sponsor a student or an elder monthly, you can stop whenever you like — we just ask for about 15 days' notice so no one is left without support unexpectedly. Money already spent cannot be returned.

Material donations and blood

Clothes, groceries and other goods cannot be returned or exchanged once handed over — they reach beneficiaries within days. Please give only clean, unworn or gently used clothing, and unexpired food. We may decline anything unsuitable. Blood donation and volunteer time involve no payment, so no refund arises.

Foreign donations

We do not hold FCRA registration and cannot legally accept donations from bank accounts outside India. If one reaches us, we will return it. Please contact us before giving from abroad.
