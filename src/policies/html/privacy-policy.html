Privacy Policy

Nallathae Nadakkum Social Trust  ·  Last updated 28 July 2026

We collect as little about you as possible, use it only to do our work, and never sell it. Here is the whole picture in about a minute.

What we collect

  • If you contact us, volunteer, or tell us about a donation: your name, phone number, email, and address if you give it. Volunteers may also share skills, availability and areas of interest.
  • If you ask for help: the details and documents needed to verify and assist your case.
  • Whenever anyone visits the site: basic technical data such as device type, browser, approximate location and pages viewed. This is statistical and does not normally identify you.

Why we use it

To reply to you, coordinate volunteers, thank donors and issue acknowledgements, run our programmes, keep the records the law requires, and improve the website. Nothing else.

What we never do

  • We never sell, rent or trade your information.
  • We never see or store your payment details. Donations are made by QR code or UPI ID, and the payment happens entirely inside your own banking app. We hold no card numbers, no bank credentials, no UPI PINs.
  • We never track or profile children.

We will never ask you for a UPI PIN, OTP, password or card number. Anyone who does is not us. Report them by phone or WhatsApp at +91 75400 17625.

Cookies

We use cookies needed to make the site work, and analytics cookies to see which pages are useful. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Pages with embedded maps, videos or social media may set their own cookies - those services have their own privacy policies.

Children

We do not knowingly collect information about anyone under 18 without a parent or guardian's consent. Photographs and stories involving children are published only with a guardian's consent, and never with a child's full name, school or exact location. More detail is in our Child Protection Policy.

How long we keep it

Only as long as we need it, or as long as the law requires. Donation and financial records must be kept for several years under Indian tax law, even if you later ask us to stop contacting you.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask us to:

  • tell you what we hold about you;
  • correct or complete anything that is wrong;
  • delete it, unless the law requires us to keep it;
  • stop sending you updates, or withdraw your consent at any time.

Contact us by phone or WhatsApp. It is free, and we will respond within a reasonable time. If you are not satisfied, you can escalate to the Data Protection Board of India.
