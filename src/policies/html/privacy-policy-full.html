1. Introduction

Nallathe Nadakkum Samuga Sevai Arakkattalai (நல்லதே நடக்கும் சமூக சேவை அறக்கட்டளை) — "Nallathe Nadakkum", "the Trust", "we", "us" or "our" — is an irrevocable public charitable trust registered in India under Deed No. 16/2025 with the Sub-Registrar, Tiruchengode Town & Taluk, Namakkal District, Tamil Nadu. We operate the website at nallathe-nadakkum-social-service-tr.vercel.app (the "Website") to share information about our mission, projects, volunteer opportunities, events, campaigns, beneficiaries and success stories, and to allow supporters to reach us.

We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains, in plain language, what information we collect when you visit or interact with our Website, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, how long we keep it, and what choices and rights you have in relation to it.

We collect only the information we reasonably need to carry out our charitable work. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.

By using the Website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. Where the law requires your consent for a particular activity, we will ask for it separately and clearly, and you may decline or later withdraw that consent.

2. Scope and Applicability

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • the Website and all pages, forms and features hosted on it;
  • personal information you voluntarily share with us by email, telephone, messaging applications or written correspondence in connection with the Website;
  • information collected automatically by our Website and by the analytics or hosting services we use; and
  • any future online services, forms or features we may add to the Website, as described in Section 15.

This Privacy Policy does not apply to:

  • third-party websites, applications or social media platforms that we link to or embed content from, each of which operates under its own privacy policy;
  • information you choose to share publicly on social media platforms; or
  • offline activities unconnected with the Website, except where we state otherwise.

3. Definitions

For ease of understanding, the following terms are used in this Policy:

  • "Personal Information" or "Personal Data" means any data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to such data.
  • "Data Principal" means the individual to whom the Personal Data relates. In the case of a child, this includes the child's parent or lawful guardian.
  • "Data Fiduciary" means the person or organisation that determines the purpose and means of processing Personal Data. For the purposes of this Website, Nallathe Nadakkum is the Data Fiduciary.
  • "Processing" means any operation performed on Personal Data, including collection, recording, storage, use, sharing, retention and erasure.
  • "Child" means an individual who has not completed eighteen (18) years of age.

4. Information We Collect

4.1 Information you provide voluntarily

We collect information only when you choose to give it to us — for example, when you write to us, express interest in volunteering, register for an event, or tell us about a donation you have made. Depending on how you interact with us, this may include:

  • Name — your full name or the name you prefer to be addressed by;
  • Email address — for correspondence, acknowledgements and updates;
  • Phone number — where you provide it so that we can contact you;
  • Postal address — where required for correspondence, receipts or delivery of materials;
  • Volunteer information — areas of interest, skills, availability, preferred location of service, languages spoken, occupation or educational background, and any other details you share when offering to volunteer with us;
  • Donation information — limited to records that you voluntarily share with us, as explained in Section 6;
  • Communication history — records of your emails, messages, enquiry forms, feedback and telephone conversations with us, together with our responses;
  • Media and content you submit — photographs, videos, testimonials, written stories or documents that you voluntarily send to us or upload, where such a facility is offered;
  • Any other information you choose to share in the course of your interaction with the Organisation.

4.2 Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our Website and its hosting and analytics providers may automatically record certain technical information when you visit. This information is generally statistical and is not ordinarily used to identify you personally. It may include:

  • Device information — device type, operating system, screen resolution and general device characteristics;
  • Browser information — browser type and version, language preference and user-agent string;
  • Connection and approximate location — Internet Protocol (IP) address, which may be truncated or anonymised by our analytics provider, and the approximate geographic region derived from it;
  • Usage data — pages visited, date and time of visit, duration, scroll and click behaviour, the page or source that referred you to us, and the page you exit from;
  • Cookies and similar technologies — as described in Section 7;
  • Server logs — records kept by our hosting provider for security, diagnostics and troubleshooting.

4.3 Information we do not intentionally collect

We do not intentionally collect sensitive information such as caste, religion, health records, biometric data, financial account credentials or government identification numbers through the Website, unless it is strictly necessary for a specific programme, beneficiary assistance, statutory reporting or grant requirement. In such cases we will tell you why it is needed and obtain your consent before collecting it.

Please never send us passwords, banking credentials, card numbers, CVV, UPI PINs or one-time passwords (OTPs). We will never ask you for them, and no representative of Nallathe Nadakkum is authorised to request them.

5. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect only for lawful, specific and limited purposes connected with our charitable objectives, including:

  • Responding to enquiries — answering your questions, requests, feedback and correspondence;
  • Volunteer coordination — assessing volunteer interest, matching volunteers to suitable projects, scheduling, briefing and staying in touch with our volunteer community;
  • Donation acknowledgements — thanking donors and confirming receipt of contributions voluntarily reported to us;
  • Issuing acknowledgements — preparing and issuing donation acknowledgements recording your contribution. The Trust's applications under Sections 12A and 80G of the Income-tax Act, 1961 are currently pending, so we cannot at present issue tax-deductible receipts;
  • Event and campaign administration — managing registrations, attendance, logistics and follow-up for our events, drives and campaigns;
  • Communication with supporters — sending updates, newsletters, invitations, impact reports and appeals to those who have asked to hear from us;
  • Improving website performance — understanding which pages and content are useful, diagnosing technical problems, and improving the design, accessibility and security of the Website;
  • Internal administration — maintaining our internal records, donor and volunteer registers, programme documentation, impact assessment and organisational reporting;
  • Legal compliance — meeting our obligations under applicable Indian laws, including accounting, taxation, audit and reporting requirements, and responding to lawful requests from courts, regulators or government authorities;
  • Protecting rights and safety — preventing fraud, misuse of the Website and impersonation of the Organisation, and protecting the safety of our staff, volunteers and beneficiaries.

We will not use your Personal Information for a materially different purpose without first informing you and, where required, obtaining your consent.

5.1 Consent and choice

Where we rely on your consent — for example, to send you newsletters or campaign updates — that consent is free, specific, informed and unambiguous, and is given by a clear affirmative action. You may withdraw your consent at any time by writing to us at the details in Section 19, or by using the unsubscribe link in our emails where one is provided. Withdrawal applies going forward and does not affect the lawfulness of processing already carried out; we may continue to retain limited records where the law requires it.

6. Donations

6.1 How donations currently work

At present, the Website does not have an integrated payment gateway. Donations are entirely voluntary and are accepted through a QR code and a UPI ID displayed on the Website. When you choose to donate, you complete the payment directly within your own UPI application, bank application or payment app.

Please note that the Website:

  • does not store payment card details;
  • does not process online payments or host any payment page;
  • does not save banking credentials, UPI PINs, passwords or one-time passwords;
  • does not capture or record your transaction as it takes place.

Your payment is processed entirely by your chosen UPI or banking application and by the payment systems operated under the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) and your bank. Those services are governed by their own terms and privacy policies, over which we have no control. We do not receive your card number, account credentials or authentication details at any time.

6.2 What we may receive or record

Through the bank statement of the Organisation's account, we may receive limited transaction information such as the transaction reference number, date, amount, and the name or UPI handle associated with the payment. This information reaches us through our bank in the ordinary course of banking, and not through the Website.

Separately, you may voluntarily share donation information with us — for example, by emailing or messaging us a payment screenshot, a transaction reference number, or your name, address, PAN and contact details so that we can acknowledge your contribution or issue a receipt. We record such information only because you have chosen to share it, and we use it only for acknowledgement, receipting, accounting, audit and statutory compliance.

6.3 Donation records and legal obligations

Records of donations and donor details form part of our books of account, which are maintained as a complete ledger and audited annually by a certified Chartered Accountant. They may need to be retained and reported in accordance with Indian law, including the Income-tax Act, 1961. We will retain such records for the period required by law even if you later ask us to stop contacting you.

The Trust does not hold registration under the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act, 2010 and is therefore legally prohibited from accepting donations from bank accounts located outside India. Please do not remit funds from an overseas account. Full details of how contributions and refunds are handled are set out in our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

6.4 Caution against fraud

Please donate only through the QR code and UPI ID published on our official Website at nallathe-nadakkum-social-service-tr.vercel.app, or communicated directly by an authorised representative of the Organisation. Verify the beneficiary name shown in your payment application before confirming any transaction. We are not responsible for donations made to accounts, links or QR codes that are not officially published by us. If you notice any suspicious page, message or account claiming to collect funds on our behalf, please inform us immediately using the details in Section 19.

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

7.1 What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website you visit. They allow the website to remember your actions and preferences over time. We may also use similar technologies such as local storage, pixels, tags and software development kits (SDKs); these are covered by this section.

7.2 Categories of cookies we may use

(a) Essential cookies. Necessary for the Website to function. They support core operations such as page navigation, load balancing, security, form submission and remembering your cookie preferences. The Website cannot operate properly without them, and they are typically not subject to consent.

(b) Analytics and performance cookies. These help us understand how visitors use the Website — which pages are viewed, how long visitors stay, and where errors occur — so that we can improve content and performance. The information is aggregated and used for statistical purposes. Where a consent mechanism is presented, these are set only if you accept them.

(c) Functional or preference cookies. These remember choices you make, such as language, region or display preferences, to give you a more convenient experience.

(d) Third-party and embedded content cookies. Pages containing embedded maps, videos or social media content may allow those third parties to set their own cookies. See Section 8.

(e) Optional marketing cookies. We do not currently use cookies for advertising or behavioural targeting. If we ever introduce them, we will update this Policy and seek your consent first.

7.3 Cookie consent

Where required by applicable law, or where we deploy non-essential cookies, we will present a cookie notice or banner allowing you to accept or decline optional categories. You may change or withdraw your cookie preferences at any time through that notice, where available, or through your browser settings.

7.4 Managing cookies through your browser

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you view existing cookies, delete them individually or entirely, block cookies from specific sites, block all third-party cookies, or browse in private or incognito mode. The option is usually found under Settings → Privacy and Security.

Please note that blocking or deleting cookies may affect the functioning of parts of the Website, and that your preferences must be set again on each browser and device you use.

7.5 Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" signal. As there is no uniform industry standard for responding to such signals, our Website does not currently respond to them. We will revisit this position if a recognised standard is adopted.

If you have consented to such publication and later wish it withdrawn, please contact us and we will make reasonable efforts to remove or anonymise the material from our own channels, though we may be unable to recall material already printed or shared by third parties.

10. Data Security

We follow reasonable security practices and procedures designed to protect Personal Information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or misuse. Depending on the nature and sensitivity of the data, these measures may include:

  • Encryption in transit — the Website is served over HTTPS/SSL, so information exchanged between your browser and our Website is encrypted;
  • Encryption or protected storage — applied where appropriate to records held electronically;
  • Limited, role-based access — access to Personal Information is restricted to authorised personnel who need it for a defined purpose;
  • Confidentiality obligations — staff, volunteers and service providers who handle Personal Information are expected to keep it confidential;
  • Access controls — password protection, account controls and, where feasible, multi-factor authentication on the accounts and systems we use;
  • Physical safeguards — secure storage of any physical records at our premises;
  • Backups and recovery — periodic backups of important records;
  • Periodic review — reviewing our practices, providers and access rights from time to time.

10.1 Limitations of internet security

While we take these steps seriously, please understand that no method of transmission over the Internet and no method of electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted to or from the Website, and any transmission is at your own risk. You are also responsible for keeping any credentials you may hold confidential and for using a secure device and network.

10.2 Data breach

In the event of a personal data breach affecting your information, we will act promptly to contain and assess the incident, and will notify affected individuals and the competent authority, including the Data Protection Board of India, in the manner and within the timelines required under applicable law.

11. Data Retention

We keep Personal Information only for as long as it is necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, or for such longer period as is required or permitted by law. In practice:

  • Enquiries and general correspondence — retained for a reasonable period after the matter is closed, so that we can maintain continuity of communication.
  • Volunteer records — retained for the duration of your association with us and for a reasonable period afterwards, for reference, recognition and safeguarding purposes.
  • Donation and financial records — retained for the period prescribed under applicable accounting, taxation and regulatory laws in India, which may extend to several years.
  • Newsletter and communication preferences — retained until you unsubscribe or withdraw consent, together with a minimal record of the withdrawal so that we can honour it.
  • Analytics and log data — retained in aggregated or de-identified form for the retention period set by the relevant service, or for as long as needed for security and performance analysis.

When Personal Information is no longer required and no legal obligation requires its retention, we will erase it, or anonymise it so that it can no longer be linked to you.

12. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the following rights in relation to your Personal Information:

  • Right to access — to ask for confirmation of whether we hold Personal Information about you, a summary of that information, and details of how it has been processed and with whom it has been shared.
  • Right to correction and completion — to have inaccurate or misleading information corrected, incomplete information completed, and outdated information updated.
  • Right to erasure — to request deletion of your Personal Information where it is no longer necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, unless retention is required by law.
  • Right to withdraw consent — to withdraw, at any time and as easily as it was given, any consent you have provided. Withdrawal applies prospectively and does not affect processing already carried out.
  • Right to grievance redressal — to raise a concern or complaint with our Grievance Officer, whose details appear in Section 19, and to receive a response within a reasonable time.
  • Right to nominate — to nominate another individual to exercise your rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity, in the manner permitted by law.
  • Right to opt out of communications — to ask us to stop sending you newsletters, appeals or campaign updates.
  • Right to escalate — if you are not satisfied with our response, to approach the Data Protection Board of India or another competent authority in accordance with applicable law.

12.1 How to exercise your rights

Please write to us at the email address given in Section 19, stating clearly the right you wish to exercise and the information you are referring to. We may ask you for details reasonably necessary to verify your identity before acting on a request, in order to protect your data from being disclosed to the wrong person. We will respond within a reasonable time and, in any case, within any period prescribed by law. These services are provided free of charge.

12.2 Your responsibilities

When exercising your rights, please provide accurate and truthful information, do not impersonate another person, and do not submit false or frivolous complaints. Where you share another person's details with us — for example, a co-volunteer or a beneficiary — please ensure you are authorised to do so.

13. Children's Privacy

Protecting children is central to the work of Nallathe Nadakkum, and we treat children's data with particular care. For the purposes of this Policy, a child is an individual who has not completed eighteen (18) years of age.

  • The Website is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect Personal Information from a child through the Website without the verifiable consent of the child's parent or lawful guardian.
  • Where we do process a child's Personal Information — for example, in connection with a programme, sponsorship, event or beneficiary story — we obtain verifiable consent from the parent or lawful guardian beforehand, and we process only the information necessary for that purpose.
  • We do not track, monitor or profile children, and we do not engage in behavioural or targeted advertising directed at children.
  • We do not knowingly undertake any processing of a child's Personal Information that is likely to have a detrimental effect on the well-being of the child.
  • Photographs, videos or stories involving children are published only with the verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian, and we avoid disclosing details — such as full names, school names or precise locations — that could put a child at risk.

Similar protections apply to persons with disabilities who have a lawful guardian, whose data we process only with the consent of that guardian.

If you believe that we have inadvertently collected Personal Information from a child without the required consent, please contact us immediately using the details in Section 19. We will verify the position promptly and delete the information where appropriate.
